# Cart photo and clear-glass sources

Additional cart data comes from public Bullseye product JSON and sitemaps, with source URLs, timestamps and SHA-256 hashes recorded in clear-cart.json and cart-photos.json. Original product photographs are copyright Bullseye Glass Co. Photos are loaded directly from Bullseye/Shopify when shown, not uploaded to any image-analysis service.

Photo precedence: exact variant-assigned image, then that grade/sheet product listing image. A shared product photo is not described as a unique jar-weight or sheet-size photograph. The existing medium-frit color-sampling guide remains unchanged and separate.
